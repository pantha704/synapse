\chapter{Work Plan}

The project is scheduled over two semesters, Project-I and Project-II, with a deliverable attached to each month.

\section{Project-I (Semester 1)}

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Work Plan for Project-I}
\label{tab:workplan1}
\begin{tabular}{|c|p{7cm}|c|}
\hline
\thead{Month} & \thead{Activity} & \thead{Deliverable} \\ \hline
Month 1 & Read across existing LMS platforms and the literature behind them. Settle the requirement list and cut it to what one team can finish. & Requirements Document \\ \hline
Month 2 & Design the architecture and schema. Stand up the Next.js project, the Supabase backend and the authentication flow. & Architecture Design \\ \hline
Month 3 & Build multi-tenant onboarding and role-based access, then the teacher dashboard. & Admin \& Teacher Modules \\ \hline
Month 4 & Build the React Flow canvas: node layout, dragging, saved positions, and the state model behind each node. & Mindmap Canvas \\ \hline
Month 5 & Build the student view and the class dashboard. Wire dual tracking from stored records rather than from derived guesses. & Student Module \& Analytics \\ \hline
\end{tabular}
\end{table}


\section{Project-II (Semester 2)}

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Work Plan for Project-II}
\label{tab:workplan2}
\begin{tabular}{|c|p{7cm}|c|}
\hline
\thead{Month} & \thead{Activity} & \thead{Deliverable} \\ \hline
Month 1 & Integrate the retrieval layer: pgvector for embeddings, PostgreSQL full-text search as the fallback, Groq for inference. & AI Retrieval Module \\ \hline
Month 2 & Build the analytics dashboards and widen engagement instrumentation beyond topic completion. & Analytics Dashboard \\ \hline
Month 3 & Run the demo with a group of students and teachers. Collect feedback on usability, legibility and trust in the assistant. & User Testing Report \\ \hline
Month 4 & Close bugs, tighten performance, finish documentation, and prepare the report and the presentation. & Final Report \\ \hline
\end{tabular}
\end{table}
