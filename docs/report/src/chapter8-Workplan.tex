\chapter{Work Plan}

The project is scheduled over two semesters, Project-I and Project-II, each divided into months with an associated deliverable.

\section{Project-I (Semester 1)}

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Work Plan for Project-I}
\label{tab:workplan1}
\begin{tabular}{|c|p{7cm}|c|}
\hline
\thead{Month} & \thead{Activity} & \thead{Deliverable} \\ \hline
Month 1 & Review existing learning management systems and the associated literature. Define the requirement list and reduce it to a scope that one team can complete. & Requirements Document \\ \hline
Month 2 & Design the architecture and database schema. Establish the Next.js project, the Supabase backend and the authentication flow. & Architecture Design \\ \hline
Month 3 & Implement multi-tenant onboarding and role-based access control, followed by the teacher dashboard. & Admin and Teacher Modules \\ \hline
Month 4 & Implement the React Flow canvas: node layout, repositioning, persisted coordinates and the state model associated with each node. & Mindmap Canvas \\ \hline
Month 5 & Implement the student view and the class dashboard. Derive dual tracking from stored records instead of from computed estimates. & Student Module and Analytics \\ \hline
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\section{Project-II (Semester 2)}

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Work Plan for Project-II}
\label{tab:workplan2}
\begin{tabular}{|c|p{7cm}|c|}
\hline
\thead{Month} & \thead{Activity} & \thead{Deliverable} \\ \hline
Month 1 & Integrate the retrieval layer: pgvector for embeddings, PostgreSQL full-text search as the fallback, and Groq for inference. & AI Retrieval Module \\ \hline
Month 2 & Implement the analytics dashboards and extend event instrumentation beyond topic completion. & Analytics Dashboard \\ \hline
Month 3 & Conduct the evaluation with a group of students and teachers. Collect observations on usability, legibility and confidence in the assistant. & User Testing Report \\ \hline
Month 4 & Resolve defects, improve performance, complete the documentation and prepare the final report and presentation. & Final Report \\ \hline
\end{tabular}
\end{table}
